Texas Records

In Texas, reports for wells drilled from 2003 on are searchable in the Texas Water Development Board's Submitted Drillers Reports, and older reports are in a TCEQ viewer. Step-by-step help is in finding Texas water well logs, and local drillers are listed in the Texas well driller directory.
